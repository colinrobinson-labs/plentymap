No account or login. Every feature works without signing in.

Location is optional. To test without it, enter the ZIP code 92101 (San Diego) when asked "Where are you?" San Diego County has the most complete free-food listings.

Plenty Map is an independent, non-government information service. It does not process benefit applications. Official sources (USDA and state agencies) are linked in the Benefits tab.

Links to outside websites open in Safari, outside the app. "Report a problem" on a listing sends only the listing, the kind of problem, an optional short note and the app language; nothing that identifies the person.
