Plenty Map helps you eat well for less. Cook cheap recipes, plan a week of groceries around what you can spend, find free food and low-price stores near you, and see what food help you could get. In 15 languages, with no account.

Plenty Map is an independent, free service. It is not a government app and does not represent any government agency. Benefit information comes from official sources, including the U.S. Department of Agriculture (fns.usda.gov) and state agencies (for example cdss.ca.gov), linked in the app. Only the agency can decide whether you qualify.

COOK
• 56 recipes with the price per serving, many under a dollar
• Sorted for what you have: stove and oven, hot plate, microwave, or no kitchen at all
• No fridge? Turn it on and every recipe that needs cold food is hidden
• Vegetarian, vegan, halal and kosher filters, plus lower sodium
• Change the servings and every amount and price updates
• Calories, protein and sodium for each serving

BUDGET
• Tell it who you are feeding and what you can spend each week
• Get seven days of breakfast, lunch and dinner and one shopping list
• Cook dinner once and eat it again for lunch, so you buy less
• Swap any meal you don't want

FIND FOOD
• Food pantries, free meals, community fridges and little free pantries
• Grocery stores that take EBT, farmers markets and restaurants in the Restaurant Meals Program
• Today's hours, who can go, and what to bring
• Map or list, sorted by distance or best value
• Listings across the U.S., most complete in San Diego County
• See something wrong? Report it in a couple of taps so the listing gets checked and fixed

BENEFITS
• A quick, private check of what SNAP (CalFresh in California), WIC and school meals could give your household
• How to apply, with links to the official office for your state
• Help for people without a home, and 2-1-1 for anything else

IN YOUR LANGUAGE
English, Español, 简体中文, 繁體中文, Tiếng Việt, Tagalog, العربية, Kreyòl ayisyen, 한국어, Русский, Português, دری, پښتو, Soomaali and Українська.

PRIVATE BY DESIGN
• No account, no sign-up, no ads, no tracking
• Your location is used only on your phone to show what is near you. It is never sent to us or anyone else.
• Your budget, saved recipes and settings stay on your phone
