# Accessibility

**Updated {{EFFECTIVE_DATE}}**

Plenty Map is for everyone, so it has to work for everyone: on old and new phones, slow connections, small and folding screens, with a screen reader or larger text, and in 15 languages, including right-to-left Arabic, Dari and Pashto.

## Our goal

We aim to meet the [Web Content Accessibility Guidelines (WCAG) 2.2](https://www.w3.org/TR/WCAG22/) at Level AA across the website and the apps.

## What we do

- **Screen readers:** buttons, links and forms have names and labels, and lists use proper headings and landmarks.
- **Keyboard:** everything works with a keyboard, with a visible focus outline.
- **Text size and zoom:** layouts adapt to larger text and zoom up to 200% without losing content, down to 280-pixel-wide folded phones.
- **Color and contrast:** text meets contrast guidelines in light and dark themes, and color is never the only way information is shown.
- **Motion:** we respect your device's reduce-motion setting.
- **Plain language:** we write short sentences in everyday words and translate into 15 languages.
- **Works offline and on slow connections:** after your first visit, the app and your area's listings are saved on your device.
- **Testing:** we check releases with automated accessibility tools and on phone, tablet, foldable and desktop screen sizes, in several languages including right-to-left ones.

## Known limits

- **The map** can be hard to use with a screen reader. Every place on the map is also in the list next to it, with its address, hours and distance, so you never need the map.
- **Translations** are being checked by native speakers. Some wording may read awkwardly until then.
- **The full Terms of Use, Privacy Policy and other legal pages** are in English only for now. The app's own privacy summary is in every language.
- **Websites we link to** (such as food banks and benefit agencies) have their own accessibility, which we don't control.

## Tell us about a barrier

If something in Plenty Map is hard or impossible for you to use, please tell us. Email [hello@plentymap.com](mailto:hello@plentymap.com) and say which screen or page, what you were trying to do, and the device or assistive technology you use, if you're comfortable sharing it. We aim to reply within 5 working days. If we can't fix it quickly, we'll help you get the information another way.
