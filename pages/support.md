# Help and Support

Plenty Map is free. There are no accounts, no ads and nothing to sign up for.

## Need food today?

- **Call 211**, free and private, any time, in many languages. In many areas you can also text your ZIP code to **898-211**.
- In San Diego County, the **San Diego Food Bank** lists free food distributions near you.
- In an emergency, call **911**.

## Common questions

**Is Plenty Map really free?**
Yes. Every feature is free, with no ads and no paid version.

**Do I need an account?**
No. Open the app and pick your language. That's it.

**Is my information safe?**
Plenty Map doesn't collect information about you. Your settings, saved places and budget stay on your own device. Read the [Privacy Policy](/privacy) for details.

**A place was closed, or the hours were wrong.**
We're sorry. Pantry hours and stock change often, so it's always worth calling ahead when a phone number is listed. Please tell us what you found (the place name and what was wrong) at [hello@plentymap.com](mailto:hello@plentymap.com), and we'll fix the listing. You don't need to tell us anything about yourself.

**How do I change the language?**
Tap the language button at the top of any screen, or **Settings → Language**. You can switch at any time.

**The app can't find my location.**
Location is optional. Type your ZIP code or neighborhood instead. To turn location on, allow it for Plenty Map in your phone's settings.

**Does it work without internet?**
Yes, mostly. After you open Plenty Map once, it keeps the app and your area's listings on your phone. Directions and calling a place still need a connection.

**How do I add Plenty Map to my home screen?**
- iPhone: open plentymap.com in Safari, tap the Share button, then **Add to Home Screen**.
- Android: open plentymap.com in Chrome, tap the menu, then **Install app** or **Add to Home screen**.

**How do I erase everything Plenty Map saved?**
Tap **Settings → Erase everything**, or uninstall the app.

**Can Plenty Map tell me if I qualify for SNAP or other help?**
Plenty Map points you to the right program and explains the basics, but only the program itself can decide if you qualify. In California, SNAP is called CalFresh.

**Is Plenty Map part of the government?**
No. Plenty Map is an independent project. We link to government and nonprofit programs but don't run them.

## For food banks, pantries and community groups

Want your program listed, or something about it corrected? Email [hello@plentymap.com](mailto:hello@plentymap.com) with your program's name, address, hours and how people can reach you.

## Contact us

Email [hello@plentymap.com](mailto:hello@plentymap.com). We read every message and usually reply within a few days. Please don't send private details such as benefit case numbers. We don't need them to help.
