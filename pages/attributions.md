## United States

- **San Diego Food Bank** and **Feeding San Diego**: food distribution sites and schedules in San Diego County, from their public food finders. Visit [sandiegofoodbank.org](https://www.sandiegofoodbank.org/get-food/) and [feedingsandiego.org](https://feedingsandiego.org/find-food/).
- **Other food banks**: where a listing says "Listed by" a food bank, its details come from that food bank's public site finder.
- **U.S. Department of Agriculture, Food and Nutrition Service**: stores, farmers markets and Restaurant Meals Program locations that accept SNAP/EBT, from the [SNAP Retailer Locator](https://www.fns.usda.gov/snap/retailer-locator) data; SNAP income limits, allotments and program rules.
- **U.S. Census Bureau**: ZIP code and place locations, from the Gazetteer Files.
- **U.S. Bureau of Labor Statistics**: average food prices.
- **USDA FoodData Central**: nutrition information.
- **U.S. Department of Health and Human Services**: poverty guidelines.
- **State agencies**: SNAP program names, rules and application links for each state, linked from the Benefits tab.

Information produced by the U.S. federal government is generally in the public domain. Nutrition data from USDA FoodData Central is published under [CC0 1.0](https://creativecommons.org/publicdomain/zero/1.0/).

## Recipes, prices and deals

Every recipe in Plenty Map was written for Plenty Map. Typical store-brand prices are estimates based on stores' public prices on the dates shown in the app. Restaurant and store deals, including kids-eat-free offers, are taken from each business's own public announcements. Store and restaurant names and brands belong to their owners and are used only to identify them. They don't endorse Plenty Map.
