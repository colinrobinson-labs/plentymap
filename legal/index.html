Not a government website

Plenty Map is an independent project. It is not part of, endorsed by, or acting for any government agency, including the U.S. Department of Agriculture and state SNAP agencies, or any food bank, store or restaurant. We link to official sources (such as usda.gov, ca.gov, gov.uk and canada.ca) so you can check our information. For a decision about your benefits, contact the agency that runs the program.

Listings and corrections

If you run a food program, store or other place in Plenty Map and want its details changed or removed, email us from your organization's address with the listing's name and what should change. We usually act within 2 working days. You can also use Report a problem on the listing.

Copyright and trademark complaints

We respect other people's rights. If you believe something in Plenty Map infringes your copyright or trademark, email hello@plentymap.com with the subject "Copyright or trademark notice" and include:

  • your name and contact details, and the organization you represent, if any;
  • what you own (the work or trademark) and where it appears in Plenty Map (a link or a description);
  • why you believe the use isn't authorized; and
  • a statement that the information in your notice is accurate, and that you are the owner or authorized to act for the owner.

We'll review it promptly and remove or correct material where appropriate. If something of yours was removed and you believe that was a mistake, you can tell us the same way.

Requests from authorities

Plenty Map holds almost no information about the people who use it: no accounts, names, locations or usage history. We respond only to valid legal process, and where the law allows, we'll say publicly how many requests we receive.

Security

If you find a security problem in Plenty Map, please email hello@plentymap.com with the subject "Security." Please give us a reasonable chance to fix it before telling others, and don't access anyone else's data or disrupt the service while testing. We'll thank you publicly if you'd like.
