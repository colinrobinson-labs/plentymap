Plenty Map is built on information shared by food banks, governments, volunteers and open-source projects. Thank you. This page credits each source and the license its data or software comes under. Map and places

OpenStreetMap. Map data, place names, and many food banks, community fridges, free pantry boxes and meal programs come from OpenStreetMap. © OpenStreetMap contributors. OpenStreetMap data is available under the Open Database License (ODbL) 1.0. Our listing files that contain OpenStreetMap data are also available under the ODbL, and the code that builds them is public at github.com/colinrobinson-labs/plentymap. You can help improve OpenStreetMap.

Protomaps. Street map tiles are built with the Protomaps basemap from OpenStreetMap data, and drawn with the Protomaps basemap styles.

United States

  • San Diego Food Bank and Feeding San Diego: food distribution sites and schedules in San Diego County, from their public food finders. Visit sandiegofoodbank.org and feedingsandiego.org.
  • Other food banks: where a listing says "Listed by" a food bank, its details come from that food bank's public site finder.
  • U.S. Department of Agriculture, Food and Nutrition Service: stores, farmers markets and Restaurant Meals Program locations that accept SNAP/EBT, from the SNAP Retailer Locator data; SNAP income limits, allotments and program rules.
  • U.S. Census Bureau: ZIP code and place locations, from the Gazetteer Files.
  • U.S. Bureau of Labor Statistics: average food prices.
  • USDA FoodData Central: nutrition information.
  • U.S. Department of Health and Human Services: poverty guidelines.
  • State agencies: SNAP program names, rules and application links for each state, linked from the Benefits tab.

Information produced by the U.S. federal government is generally in the public domain. Nutrition data from USDA FoodData Central is published under CC0 1.0.

Canada

These sources are used as Plenty Map opens in Canada.

  • Province of British Columbia: food bank locations from DataBC. Contains information licensed under the Open Government Licence – British Columbia.
  • City of Vancouver: free and low-cost food programs. Contains information licensed under the Open Government Licence – Vancouver.

Recipes, prices and deals

Every recipe in Plenty Map was written for Plenty Map. Typical store-brand prices are estimates based on stores' public prices on the dates shown in the app. Restaurant and store deals, including kids-eat-free offers, are taken from each business's own public announcements. Store and restaurant names and brands belong to their owners and are used only to identify them. They don't endorse Plenty Map.
