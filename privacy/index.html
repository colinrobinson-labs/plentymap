Privacy Policy

Effective November 18, 2026

Plenty Map is a free app and website that helps people find free food and cheap groceries, cook on a budget, and get help with programs like SNAP and 211. It is an independent project based in San Diego, California.

The short version: Plenty Map has no accounts and no ads. We don't collect, store, sell or share information about you. What you type and choose in the app stays on your phone or computer.

What we collect

Nothing that identifies you. Specifically, Plenty Map does not ask for or collect:

  • your name, email address, phone number or any account login
  • your location (see "Your location" below)
  • your income, benefits, household details or budget numbers
  • what you search for, save or look at

There are no advertising, analytics or tracking tools in the app, and the website sets no tracking cookies.

What stays on your device

To be useful, Plenty Map remembers a few things on your own device only:

  • the language you picked
  • your settings, such as your cooking equipment, weekly food budget and how much you like to cook
  • places and recipes you save, and numbers you enter in the Budget tab

This information is never sent to us or anyone else. You can erase it at any time with Settings → Erase everything in the app, or by uninstalling the app or clearing this site's data in your browser.

Your location

If you allow it, Plenty Map uses your location to sort food help by distance. That happens on your device; your location is not sent to us or to any map company. You can say no and type a ZIP code or neighborhood instead, which is also looked up on your device.

When you ask for directions, Plenty Map opens your phone's maps app with the destination address. From then on, that maps app's own privacy policy applies.

What our web host sees

The app, its maps and its food listings are served by our web host, GitHub Pages. Like any website, the host receives basic technical details needed to deliver those files: your IP address, your device and browser type, the time, and which file was requested. Listings are packaged by region, so a request shows at most the general region whose data you downloaded, never your exact location.

We do not use this information to identify you, and we have no access to logs of it. GitHub may keep short-term logs for security and to keep the service running, as described in the GitHub Privacy Statement.

Links to other services

Plenty Map links to food banks, pantries, 211, SNAP (CalFresh in California) and other programs. When you tap a link, call a number or visit their site, you leave Plenty Map and their own privacy policies apply. We never send them anything about you.

If you contact us

If you email us, we receive your email address and whatever you write. We use it only to answer you. We don't add you to any list, and we don't share your message.

The "Something wrong here?" buttons on a food site open your own email app with a message already written: the site's name, address, listing number and the problem you picked. Nothing is sent until you press send, and the app records nothing about it. We use these reports only to check and fix listings, and we may pass the site details (never your email address) to the food bank that runs the site.

Children

Plenty Map is made for families, including children and teens. Because we don't collect personal information from anyone, we don't collect it from children under 13 either.

Selling and sharing

We do not sell or share personal information, as those words are used in California law, and we never have. We don't track you across apps or websites. We treat Global Privacy Control and Do Not Track signals as already honored, because there is no tracking to turn off.

Your choices and rights

You can use every part of Plenty Map without giving us any personal information. Since we hold no information about you, there is nothing for us to look up, correct or delete. If you have a question anyway, email us and we'll answer.

Changes to this policy

If this policy changes, we'll post the new version here with a new effective date. If Plenty Map ever needs to collect something new, the app will ask you first and explain why. It won't happen quietly.

Translations

This policy is available in every language Plenty Map supports. If a translation and the English version differ, the English version is the reference.
