Privacy Policy

Effective November 19, 2026

The short version

  • No accounts, no ads, no tracking. There are no analytics, advertising or tracking tools in Plenty Map, no tracking cookies, and we never sell or share information about you.
  • What you enter stays on your device. Your language, area, budget, saved places, recipes and shopping list are kept on your phone or computer, not sent to us.
  • Your location never leaves your device. Plenty Map uses it there to sort places by distance.
  • We receive very little: the problem reports you choose to send (with no name or contact details), emails you choose to write, and the basic technical details any website's servers see when they send you a page.

The rest of this policy explains each of these in detail, and your rights under the laws of California and other US states, the UK, the European Union and Canada.

1. Who we are

Plenty Map ("Plenty Map," "we," "us") is a free app and website that helps people eat well for less: find free food and low-cost groceries, plan meals and a budget, cook, and learn about food benefits. It is an independent project based in San Diego, California, USA.

For the purposes of data protection law, Plenty Map is the "controller" of the small amount of personal information described here (the "business" under California law). You can reach us at hello@plentymap.com. Our privacy lead reads every message sent to that address.

This policy covers the Plenty Map website (plentymap.com), the Plenty Map apps for iPhone and Android, and the problem-report service at report.plentymap.com (together, the "Service").

2. What we don't collect

Plenty Map is built so we don't need to know who you are. We don't ask for, collect or keep:

  • your name, email address, phone number or any account login (there are no accounts);
  • your location, ZIP code or postcode;
  • your income, household, benefits, health, immigration status, budget or anything you type into the benefits quick check;
  • what you search for, view, save or put on your shopping list;
  • advertising IDs, device fingerprints, or tracking or analytics data of any kind.

There are no third-party advertising, analytics, crash-reporting, social media or tracking tools in the website or the apps. The apps and website load nothing from any other company's servers.

3. What stays on your device

To be useful, Plenty Map remembers some things on your own device only, using your browser's or app's local storage:

  • the language and settings you choose (such as theme, kitchen equipment and how much you like to cook);
  • the area you choose for listings;
  • your weekly budget, meal plan, shopping list, prices you edit, and saved places and recipes;
  • copies of the app and of the listings for your area, so it works offline.

This information is never sent to us or anyone else. We store it only because you asked the app to remember it, so no cookie banner is needed. You can erase all of it at any time with Settings → Erase everything, by clearing this site's data in your browser, or by uninstalling the app.

4. Your location

If you allow it, Plenty Map uses your device's location to sort places by distance and show the right listings for your area. This happens entirely on your device. Your location is not sent to us, to our hosting providers, or to any map company.

To show the listings for your area, the app downloads the listing file for a large area (about 50 km, or 30 miles, across) from our servers. That request reveals at most the general area whose listings you downloaded, never your exact location. If you'd rather not share your location, type a ZIP code, postcode or town instead. That is also looked up on your device, using lookup files that cover many places at once (for example, every ZIP code starting with the same three digits).

You can turn location access off at any time in your phone or browser settings.

When you tap Directions, Plenty Map opens your phone's maps app (such as Apple Maps or Google Maps) with only the destination's address. From then on, that app's own privacy policy applies.

5. What our hosting providers see when you use the Service

Like every website and online app, Plenty Map has to send files to your device over the internet. When it does, the servers delivering those files receive the technical details needed to do that: your device's IP address, the type of device and browser, the time, and which file was requested (for example, the listings for a region, or a piece of the map).

  • Cloudflare, Inc. delivers our website, app files and map tiles (tiles.plentymap.com), and protects them from attacks.
  • GitHub, Inc. stores our public source code and may deliver some website files while we finish moving our hosting to Cloudflare.

These providers act on our behalf. They keep short-lived logs of these requests for security and to keep their service running, as described in the Cloudflare Privacy Policy and the GitHub Privacy Statement. We don't access or use these logs to identify or track anyone, and we haven't turned on any analytics, logging exports or tracking features. We don't set cookies, and we've left off the provider features that would set them (such as bot challenges).

6. Problem reports

Every listing has a Report a problem button. If you use it, we receive only:

  • which listing it's about (our listing number);
  • the problem you picked (for example, "closed" or "wrong hours");
  • the note you write, if you choose to write one (up to 280 characters);
  • your app language, so we can read your note; and
  • the date we received it (no time of day).

We don't receive your name, contact details, location, device information or account details with a report, and our report service doesn't store your IP address or browser details. To stop abuse, Cloudflare counts requests per connection for about one minute. That count isn't stored or seen by us.

Please don't put personal information in the note. Email addresses, phone numbers and links are removed from notes automatically, and if anything else personal gets through, we'll delete it when we find it. Nothing is sent until you tap Send.

We use reports only to check and fix listings. A listing with reports may be checked automatically against its source and flagged or hidden. We publish a simple list of listing numbers and their status so the app can hide closed places, and a public summary of the number and type of reports per listing. Your note is never published and is visible only to the person who runs Plenty Map. We may tell the organization that runs a listing what kind of problem was reported, but never anything about you.

How long we keep reports: notes are erased 90 days after a report arrives, and whole reports are deleted after 180 days.

7. If you email us

If you email us, we receive your email address, your name if your email shows it, and whatever you write. We use it only to answer you and to fix what you told us about. We don't add you to any mailing list. Our email is hosted by Apple (iCloud Mail), which processes it on our behalf. We delete messages when we no longer need them, and at most two years after our last reply, unless the law requires us to keep them.

Please don't send us private details such as benefit case numbers, health details or immigration information. We don't need them to help you.

8. App stores

If you download Plenty Map from Apple's App Store or Google Play, Apple or Google handles the download under its own privacy policy. They may share with us aggregated statistics, such as the number of downloads per country, and crash reports, but only if you have agreed to share them in your device settings. These don't tell us who you are.

9. Other websites, apps and phone calls

Plenty Map links to food banks, pantries, government agencies, 211, stores and other services, and can open your phone's dialer, maps app, share sheet or browser. When you use one of these, you leave Plenty Map and that service's own privacy policy applies. We never send them information about you.

10. How we use information, and our legal reasons

We use the limited information above only to:

  • deliver and protect the Service (hosting logs kept by our providers). Legal basis in the UK and EU: our legitimate interest in running a secure, working service.
  • fix listings (problem reports). Legal basis: our legitimate interest, and the public's, in accurate information about where to find food.
  • answer you (emails). Legal basis: our legitimate interest in replying to people who contact us, or taking steps you asked for.
  • meet legal obligations, such as responding to valid legal requests.

We don't use any information for advertising, profiling, or automated decisions about you.

11. Who we share information with

We share information only with the service providers named above (Cloudflare, GitHub and Apple for email), who process it for us under contracts that require them to protect it, and when the law requires us to, such as a valid court order. Because we keep so little, there's almost nothing we could share. If Plenty Map is ever transferred to a new organization, such as a nonprofit formed to run it, this policy will continue to protect the information described here.

We don't sell or share personal information, as "sell" and "share" are defined in California law, and we never have. We don't use personal information for targeted advertising or cross-context behavioral advertising, and we don't track you across apps or websites. Because there's no tracking to turn off, we already treat Global Privacy Control and Do Not Track signals as honored.

12. International data transfers

Plenty Map is run from the United States, and our service providers may process information in the United States and other countries. When information from the UK, European Economic Area or Switzerland goes to the United States, our providers rely on the EU–US Data Privacy Framework (and its UK Extension and Swiss counterpart), the European Commission's Standard Contractual Clauses, or another lawful transfer method.

13. How we protect information

The Service uses encrypted connections (HTTPS) everywhere. The website blocks outside scripts through a strict content security policy. Report notes can be read only by the person who runs Plenty Map. The best protection, though, is design: we don't collect what we don't need.

14. Children

Plenty Map is a general-audience service, meant for adults and families. It isn't directed to children under 13, and we don't knowingly collect personal information from children under 13 (or under 16 in the UK and EU, where a younger age doesn't apply). Children can use Plenty Map's features without giving us any information. If you believe a child has sent us personal information in a report note or email, contact us and we'll delete it.

15. Your privacy rights

Depending on where you live, you may have the right to:

  • know what personal information we hold about you and get a copy;
  • correct it;
  • delete it;
  • restrict or object to how we use it;
  • receive it in a portable format;
  • opt out of its sale, sharing, targeted advertising or profiling (we don't do any of these); and
  • not be treated differently for using your rights.

You can make a request by emailing hello@plentymap.com. You may also use an authorized agent. We'll answer within the time the law requires (usually 30 to 45 days).

Because we don't keep accounts, names or contact details (except in emails you send us), we usually can't connect any information to you. For example, problem reports have nothing that identifies who sent them. We'll tell you if that's the case. If you emailed us, we can find, correct or delete that email.

Where to complain. If you're unhappy with how we handle your information, please tell us first. You also have the right to complain to a data protection authority:

  • United Kingdom: the Information Commissioner's Office (ico.org.uk)
  • European Union: the data protection authority in your country
  • Canada: the Office of the Privacy Commissioner of Canada (priv.gc.ca), or in Quebec, the Commission d'accès à l'information (cai.gouv.qc.ca)
  • California: the California Privacy Protection Agency (cppa.ca.gov)

16. More for California residents

In the past 12 months, the only personal information involved in the Service was:

  • Identifiers and internet activity: IP addresses and device and browser types, received by our hosting providers to deliver and protect the Service, and kept in their short-lived logs;
  • Your messages: emails you sent us, and any personal information you chose to put in a problem-report note.

We got this information from you and your device. We used it only for the purposes in section 10, shared it only with the service providers in section 11, and keep it only as long as described in sections 5 to 7. We don't collect "sensitive personal information" as California law defines it. We haven't sold or shared personal information, and we don't knowingly sell or share the personal information of anyone under 16. We don't disclose personal information to others for their direct marketing.

17. Health and benefits information

The benefits quick check, your budget and any diet or health preferences you choose are processed only on your device. We don't collect "consumer health data" under laws such as Washington's My Health My Data Act, and nothing about which programs you look at is sent to us.

18. Changes to this policy

If this policy changes, we'll post the new version here with a new effective date. If we ever want to collect something new, we'll explain it here and in the app first, and ask your permission where the law requires it. It won't happen quietly.

19. Translations

This policy is written in English. We may offer translations. If a translation and the English version differ, the English version applies, unless the law where you live requires otherwise.
